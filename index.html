Areas of Expertise

Grounded in constitutional scholarship, Aditya Kumar Singh's research spans the intersection of law, governance, human rights, and technology.

⚖️

Criminal Law

Specialized interest in criminal law, criminal justice administration, principles of criminal liability, procedural safeguards, rights of the accused, victim justice, and the evolving framework of criminal jurisprudence..

🔍

Constitutional Law

Deep expertise in fundamental rights, constitutional morality, separation of powers, federalism, and judicial review – with extensive peer-reviewed output in leading law journals.

🏛️

Administrative Law

Examines judicial control of administration, delegated legislation, administrative tribunals, and the evolving landscape of regulatory governance in India.

🌐

Social Justice

Cyber Law & Technology

Right to Information
